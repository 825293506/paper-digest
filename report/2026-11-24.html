论文解决的问题

核心方法与结构
将超表面建模为介质衬底上的谐振粒子阵列，使用全极化率张量和分层介质的并矢格林函数，通过离散偶极近似（DDA）方程求解，结合伴随拓扑优化实现空间拓扑逆向设计。
关键结果
建模精度与商业电磁求解器相当，计算速度提升高达两个数量级；设计了双偏振、多频复用和多频手性解耦超表面，并进行了数值和实验验证。

仿真方法环节：DDA+伴随优化能高效处理元胞间耦合和界面效应，直接针对卡点2（有限周期相位坍缩）和卡点3（液晶贡献不足导致敏感）。

  • 伴随拓扑优化框架可用于优化TiO2纳米柱阵列，在给定液晶调谐范围内最大化相位覆盖

ABSTRACT Metasurfaces enable diverse counterintuitive electromagnetic (EM) phenomena through engineered meta‐atom arrangements, making inverse design essential for their automated exploration and optimization. However, conventional methods either require costly full‐wave simulations at each iteration or employ equivalent models that neglect inter‐meta‐atom coupling and dielectric interface effects, compromising prediction accuracy and design feasibility. Here, the metasurface is modeled as an array of resonant particles on a dielectric substrate, described using full polarizability tensors and the dyadic Green's function for a planar stratified medium. By solving the discrete dipole approximation (DDA) equations, the global EM response arising from inter‐meta‐atom coupling and dielectric interface effects is captured. Building on this model, an adjoint‐based discrete‐state topology optimization framework is developed for efficient spatial topology inverse design of metasurface arrays, enabling multidimensional, multi‐degree‐of‐freedom EM wave control. Abstract Diamond Raman lasers provide a powerful and versatile route to achieving high-power coherent emission across a broad spectral range. However, their dependence on complex and bulky free-space optics has posed a significant barrier to miniaturization and system level integration. In this work, we overcome this limitation by integrating a metasurface to enable an arbitrary wavefront control of a diamond Raman laser. This hybrid platform demonstrates remarkable versatility, supporting a diverse range of advanced functionalities, from grayscale display and multichannel vortex beam generation to dual-focus output with tunable intensity ratios and multiplane holography. Our approach uniquely combines the power scalability, coherence, and spectral coverage of state-of-the-art diamond lasers with the precise, ultracompact wavefront manipulation enabled by metasurfaces. By doing so, this work provides a viable route to bridging the long-standing gap between high-power laser generation and wavefront engineering, opening new avenues for transformative applications in compact communication, sensing, and imaging systems.
